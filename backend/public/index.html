  • RAM: 8 GB (16 GB recommended)

Installation Guide

Step-by-step setup instructions
1

Download & Extract

Download the .zip archive. Once downloaded, right-click the file and select "Extract All..." to uncompress the folder.

2

Launch Simulation

Open the extracted folder and double-click PRJ381.exe to start exploring.

3

Windows Defender / SmartScreen Prompt

If Windows displays a "Windows protected your PC" popup, click "More info" and then select "Run anyway".

1

Download APK

Download the .apk package directly on your mobile device or transfer it via USB.

2

Enable Unknown Apps Permission

When opening the file, if prompted by Android, tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source".

3

Install & Launch

Tap Install. Once finished, launch Virtual Campus Open Day from your app drawer.

1

Option A: 1-Click ADB Installer (Recommended)

Connect your Quest headset to your PC with a USB-C cable (ensure Developer Mode is enabled in the Meta Horizon mobile app). Run Install_Quest.bat to automatically install the build.

2

Option B: SideQuest Sideloading

Open SideQuest on PC, connect your Quest headset, and drag-and-drop the PRJ381-Quest3.apk onto the SideQuest title bar to install.

3

Launch from Unknown Sources

Put on your headset, open your App Library, select the filter dropdown in the top-right, choose "Unknown Sources", and tap PRJ381.
